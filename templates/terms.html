1. What Weekora is

Weekora is a marketplace that connects travellers with independent trip organisers. Weekora does not run trips itself. Each trip is planned, priced and operated by the organiser who lists it, and your arrangement for that trip is with the organiser.

2. Your account

  • You must be at least 18 years old to create an account.
  • Give accurate information and keep it up to date.
  • Keep your password private. You are responsible for activity on your account.
  • Choose the right role: traveller to discover trips, or organiser to list them.

3. For travellers

  • Read the itinerary, price, inclusions and organiser details before you commit to a trip.
  • Follow the organiser's safety instructions and local laws during the trip.
  • Cancellations, refunds and changes follow the policy set by the organiser for that trip.

4. For organisers

  • List only trips you are able and authorised to run, with accurate dates, prices and itineraries.
  • Hold any licences, permits and insurance your trips need.
  • State clearly what the price includes and your cancellation and refund policy.
  • Respond to travellers honestly and keep the trip details on Weekora current.
