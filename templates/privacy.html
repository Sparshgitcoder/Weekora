Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

This is a general template for the Weekora learning project. Have it reviewed by a qualified legal professional before real users rely on it.

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what information Weekora collects, how we use it and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address, password (stored securely, never in plain text) and whether you are a traveller or an organiser.
  • Trip listings: for organisers, the trip details you publish, such as destinations, dates, prices and descriptions.
  • Usage information: basic technical data such as your browser type and the pages you visit, used to keep the site working.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account and keep you signed in.
  • To show trip listings and connect travellers with organisers.
  • To keep Weekora safe, prevent misuse and fix problems.
  • To contact you about your account or important changes to the service.

3. When we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only:

  • with an organiser when you choose to enquire about or join their trip;
  • with service providers who help us run the website, under confidentiality obligations;
  • when required by law or to protect the safety of our users.

4. Cookies

We use essential cookies to keep you signed in and to remember basic preferences. We do not use advertising cookies.

5. How long we keep information

We keep your information while your account is active. If you delete your account, we remove your personal information unless we need to keep some of it to meet legal obligations.

6. Your choices and rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your personal information, in line with applicable data protection laws, including India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

7. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, including secure password storage. No online service can guarantee complete security, so please use a strong, unique password.

8. Children

Weekora is meant for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Weekora grows. When we make significant changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.

10. Contact

Questions about your privacy? Contact details will be added here before launch.
